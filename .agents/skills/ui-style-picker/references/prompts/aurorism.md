```text
Apply the **Aurorism** design style to the component provided below.

Do NOT change:
• layout
• HTML/JSX structure
• component hierarchy
• text labels
• functionality

Modify ONLY the aesthetic layer:
colors, shadows, gradients, radii, lighting, surfaces, and typography tone.

Follow these exact Aurorism rules:

GENERAL STYLE
• The design must feel atmospheric, luminous, and ethereal — inspired by aurora borealis light waves.
• Use soft, blended gradients with flowing motion, layered color fields, or sweeping light arcs.
• The interface should look calm, airy, and glowing — NEVER harsh, glossy, or textured.

GRADIENT SYSTEM (AURORA-LIKE)
Use multi-tone gradients consisting of cool-to-warm or cool-to-cool blends:

Recommended aurora palettes:
• Cyan (#00E5FF) → Blue (#3D5AFE) → Purple (#8E24AA)
• Teal (#1DE9B6) → Aqua (#00E5FF)
• Magenta (#D500F9) → Violet (#7B61FF)

Rules:
• Gradients must be smooth, soft, and atmospheric.
• Use flowing, wave-like or diagonal orientations (NOT rigid linear bars).
• Avoid abrupt transitions or geometric separations.

LIGHTING & GLOW
• Apply soft, ambient glows around key areas (very low-opacity).
• Add luminous haze behind major elements using blurred gradient overlays.
• Glow should be atmospheric, NOT neon or "cyberpunk."
• No sharp highlights or specular reflections.

SHADOWS
• Use extremely soft shadows or NO shadows.
• If used, shadows must be low-contrast, diffused, and purely supportive:
  Example:
  0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)
• DO NOT use hard shadows, inset shadows, or directional lighting.

SURFACES
• Surfaces should be semi-flat with gentle radiance from gradients.
• Use minimal radii (8px–16px) — avoid exaggerated pills or ultra-sharp corners.
• Panels may include subtle gradient overlays aligned with the aurora's direction.

COLOR PALETTE
• Cool-spectrum primary colors: cyan, teal, blue, violet, magenta.
• Allow secondary warm accents ONLY when softly blended.
• No flat monochrome areas unless used as contrast.
• Avoid grey-heavy or desaturated palettes.

TYPOGRAPHY
• Clean, modern sans-serif fonts.
• Text color should be high-contrast white (#FFFFFF) or soft black (#1A1A1A) depending on background.
• No text shadows unless extremely subtle atmospheric haze:
  Example: 0px 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use glass blur or transparency (that becomes Glassmorphism).
• DO NOT use matte molded surfaces or dual shadows (that becomes Neumorphism).
• DO NOT use glossy, wet, or liquid textures (that becomes Liquid/Gooey).
• DO NOT use inflatable or chunky shapes (that becomes Claymorphism).
• DO NOT use harsh neon glow (that becomes Cyberpunk/Neon UI).
• DO NOT use flat geometric blocks without gradients (that becomes Hyperflat).

The output must always reflect true Aurorism:
soft atmospheric gradients, flowing color bands, gentle glows, ethereal ambience, and elegant minimal shapes.
```

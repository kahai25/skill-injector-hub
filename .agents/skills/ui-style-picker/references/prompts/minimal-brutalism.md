Apply the **Minimal Brutalism** design style to the component provided below.

Do NOT change:
• layout
• HTML/JSX structure

Follow these exact Minimal Brutalism rules:

BASE
• Use a stark, high-contrast color palette: pure black (#000), pure white (#FFF), and 1 accent color only if needed.
• No gradients. No shadows. No glow. No transparency.
• All surfaces must be flat, hard, and unapologetically simple.
• The interface should feel raw, utilitarian, and unstyled by default.

SHAPE
• Use sharp corners (0px border-radius everywhere).
• Boxes must feel rigid and grid-aligned.
• Generous spacing is encouraged, but must stay geometric and monotone.

BORDERS & DIVIDERS
• Use 1px or 2px **solid black** borders for structure.
• Avoid rounded, dotted, dashed, or decorative lines.
• Borders may be used to mimic "unstyled HTML," but with intentional composition.

TYPOGRAPHY
• Use bold, heavy, and oversized typography for headings.
• Body text should be regular weight with high contrast.
• No decorative fonts. Use strong, simple, modern sans-serif.
• LEFT alignment preferred; avoid centered text unless required.

COLOR PALETTE
• Only black (#000), white (#FFF), and optionally **one** bold accent color.
• Accent must be fully saturated (e.g., red #FF0000, blue #0057FF, yellow #FFD500).
• Do NOT use gradients, shadows, pastels, greys, or subtle tones.

SPACING
• Brutalism embraces intentional awkwardness:
  - Large margins
  - Hard separations
  - Clear, modular blocks
• Do not soften spacing or round anything.

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use shadows or depth of any kind.
• DO NOT use rounded corners.
• DO NOT blur anything.
• DO NOT use gradients, glass, or transparency.
• DO NOT introduce color softness or subtle palette shifts.
• DO NOT apply Neumorphism, Soft UI, Glassmorphism, or Claymorphism elements.
• DO NOT add icons unless already part of the component.

The output must always reflect true Minimal Brutalism:
raw, blunt shapes, high contrast, unpolished energy, oversized type, zero decoration, and strict flatness.

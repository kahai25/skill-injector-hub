Follow these exact Retrofuturism rules:

GENERAL STYLE
• Blend nostalgic visual language with imagined "future tech" from past eras.
• Use retro color schemes, grain, worn textures, screen noise, and stylized UI shapes.
• The design may lean into either:
  1. **Vintage Pastel Futurism** (muted, soft, aged)
  2. **Dark Arcade Futurism** (neon, gritty, high contrast)
• The retro quality must be unmistakable.

COLOR PALETTES
Choose ONE of the two canonical branches:

1. **Vintage Pastel / Print Retrofuturism**
   • Sepia: #D6C4A0, #BFA980
   • Muted Red: #C06C5A
   • Faded Teal: #6FA79A
   • Warm Pastel Yellow: #F4DFA6
   • Desaturated Navy: #2F3B52

2. **Dark Arcade / Tech Retrofuturism**
   • Deep Charcoal: #0E0E12, #16161C
   • Neon Red: #FF3B30, #FF5A5F
   • Amber Orange: #F39C12
   • CRT Green: #6BFF8E
   • Electric Cyan: #00F6FF

Rules:
• Colors should evoke aged film, retro print, arcade CRT glow, or old sci-fi posters.
• Do NOT use hypermodern gradients unless they feel screen-distorted or retro-analog.

TYPOGRAPHY (STYLE-SPECIFIC)
Use fonts inspired by vintage tech, posters, arcades, or pulp sci-fi.

Recommended font styles:
• **Pixel / 16-bit fonts** for arcade retro  
• **Condensed industrial display fonts**  
• **Angular sci-fi typefaces**  
• **Retro serif display fonts ('60s sci-fi posters)**  
• **Wide tracking + ALL CAPS** for titles  
• **Monospace** for data-like text

Rules:
• Titles should feel bold, stylized, or futuristic-retro.
• Body text may be monospace or lightly condensed sans-serif.
• Avoid hyper-sleek modern fonts (they break the retro illusion).

SURFACES & TEXTURES
Apply at least one of the following texture techniques:

• Film grain overlay (subtle noise)
• Halftone or print-style shading
• Paper-like texture for light-mode retrofuturism
• CRT-like distortion for dark-mode retrofuturism
• Glitches, scan lines, micro-scratches

Recommended:
Use pseudo-elements or layered gradients to simulate aging/wear.

UI ELEMENTS (ANALOG + EARLY DIGITAL INSPIRATION)
Incorporate retro-tech shapes such as:

• Rounded-rect control panels  
• Analog dials or tuner-like elements  
• Framed info boxes with simple strokes or outlines  
• LED-like or pixel-like indicators  
• Hard-edged buttons with vintage UI framing  

AVOID:
• Modern neumorphic softness  
• Plastic-like claymorphism geometry  
• Glassy translucency  
• Liquid blobs  

LIGHTING & SHADOWS
• Shadows should be minimal and stylistic — not realistic or soft UI.
• If used, shadows must feel printed, screened, or digitally imperfect.
• Optional neon glow for dark-mode retrofuturism:
  Example: 0px 0px 12px rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.35)

COMPOSITION
• Use layout techniques inspired by vintage print design:
  - Asymmetric compositions  
  - Overlapping panels  
  - Poster-like framing  
  - Thick strokes or hard borders  
  - Label-style tags and micro-text  

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use glassmorphic blur or transparency.
• DO NOT use soft neumorphic shadows.
• DO NOT use inflated clay-like forms.
• DO NOT use ultra-modern gradients without retro texture.
• DO NOT use pristine, textureless surfaces.
• DO NOT use smooth, futuristic tech fonts.

The output must always reflect true Retrofuturism:
retro color systems, analog-inspired UI elements, era-specific typography, visible grain or wear, and a nostalgic vision of "the future."

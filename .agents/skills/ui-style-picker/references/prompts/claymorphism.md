BASE
• All components must appear as soft, inflated, 3D clay-like shapes.

• Corner radius must be large (20px–32px minimum).

  0px 6px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18)

LIGHTING & HIGHLIGHTS
• Apply subtle, soft highlight gradients to mimic clay-like curvature.
• Optional: top-edge highlights to reinforce the inflated shape.
• No sharp specular highlights (not plastic or glossy—keep it matte).

COLOR
• Vibrant, cute, friendly colors encouraged.
• Recommended: blues, pinks, purples, oranges, greens—saturated but soft.
• Use simple 2–3 color gradients for added curvature.
• Do NOT use monochrome or dull palettes (that becomes Soft UI or minimalism).

TYPOGRAPHY
• Medium or semi-bold weights.
• Round, friendly fonts preferred.
• Text color should contrast strongly with the clay surface.
• Avoid low-contrast greys or washed-out text.

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use inner shadows (not part of Claymorphism).
• DO NOT use transparency, glass, or blur.
• DO NOT use thin or sharp corners.
• DO NOT use desaturated or grayscale palettes.
• DO NOT use realistic textures (this is playful, not skeuomorphic).

soft, inflated, 3D clay-like shapes with bright colors, bold shadows, and a playful, friendly aesthetic.

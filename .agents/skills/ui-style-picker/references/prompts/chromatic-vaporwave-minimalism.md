GENERAL STYLE
• Blend retro-futuristic neon colors with minimalist, clean UI structure.

COLOR PALETTE (VAPORWAVE SIGNATURE)
Use bold retro tones such as:

• Hot Pink: #FF4FBE  
• Neon Cyan: #00F0FF  
• Electric Purple: #9B5BFF  
• Soft Magenta: #FF7AD9  
• Sunset Orange: #FF6A3D

Rules:
• Use neon hues sparingly but meaningfully.

GRADIENT SYSTEM
• Use multi-stop gradients in directions like:
  - diagonal  
  - radial  
  - sweeping horizontal "sunset" blends  

Recommended combinations:
• Pink → Purple → Blue  
• Orange → Pink  
• Cyan → Violet  

Gradients should feel atmospheric, not metallic or glass-like.

GLOW & LIGHTING
• Apply soft neon glow to key elements (buttons, accents, icons).
• Glow should be airy and aesthetic, not harsh or overpowering:
  Example: 0px 0px 12px rgba(255, 79, 190, 0.45)
• Avoid realistic lighting — this is synthetic, not skeuomorphic.

SHADOWS
• Use minimal or no shadows.  
• If used, shadows should be neon-tinted and extremely soft:
  Example: 0px 4px 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)

SURFACES
• Flat or gently gradient surfaces.
• No texture, no blur, no glassy transparency.
• Rounded corners allowed (6px–16px), but shapes should stay clean and geometric.

TYPOGRAPHY

• Neon or pastel highlights acceptable.
• No embossed/debossed effects.
• No dramatic contrast shadows — keep it minimal.

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use transparency or frosted blur (that becomes Glassmorphism).
• DO NOT use dual shadows or carved surfaces (that becomes Neumorphism).
• DO NOT use heavy 3D textures (that becomes Skeuomorphic).
• DO NOT use toy-like inflated shapes (that becomes Claymorphism).
• DO NOT introduce chaotic glitch effects (this is Vaporwave Minimalism, not full Vaporwave).
• DO NOT clutter the layout — minimal structure is required.

The output must always reflect true Chromatic Vaporwave Minimalism:
neon gradients, soft glow, retro-futuristic palette, clean minimal layout, and a dreamy, synthetic aesthetic.

```text
Apply the **Kinetic Minimalism** design style to the component provided below.

Do NOT change:
• layout
• HTML/JSX structure
• component hierarchy
• text labels
• functionality

Modify ONLY the aesthetic layer:
colors, shadows, gradients, radii, lighting, surfaces, and typography tone.

Follow these exact Kinetic Minimalism rules:

GENERAL STYLE
• The component should feel clean and minimal, but infused with a strong sense of direction, motion, or energy.
• Use angled shapes, asymmetric accents, or directional gradients to imply movement.
• Maintain simplicity—no clutter, no extraneous decoration, no heavy layering.

SURFACES
• Surfaces should be mostly flat or lightly gradient-washed.
• Use clean color blocking with clear visual hierarchy.
• Avoid texture or noise—keep everything crisp and modern.

COLOR & ACCENTING
• Use a primarily neutral minimal palette (white, black, greys, off-whites).
• Add one strong directional accent color (e.g., electric blue, vivid red, sharp yellow, or neon green).
• Accent color should drive motion: angled blocks, streaks, slashes, or directional gradients.

DIRECTIONAL ELEMENTS (VERY IMPORTANT)
• Add at least one of the following to imply kinetic energy:
  - Diagonal edges
  - Angled dividing lines
  - Motion streaks or elongated shapes
  - Directional shadows
  - Gradients with obvious vector flow
• These elements must be subtle yet clearly directional.

SHADOWS
• Shadows should be minimal, crisp, and directional—not soft or diffused.
• Use a single offset shadow:
  Example:
  4px 8px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)
• Shadow direction MUST align with the design's motion direction.

TYPOGRAPHY
• Use clean, modern sans-serif fonts.
• Prefer tight letterspacing and strong visual rhythm.
• Headings may be bold; body text should stay minimal and unobtrusive.

SHAPE & RADII
• Corners can be sharp or slightly rounded depending on the directionality.
• Avoid overly soft shapes—movements should feel intentional and dynamic.

STRICT FORBIDDEN RULES
• DO NOT use glossy, glassy, or translucent surfaces (that becomes Glassmorphism).
• DO NOT use soft diffused shadows or dual shadows (that becomes Soft UI or Neumorphism).
• DO NOT use inflated shapes (that becomes Claymorphism).
• DO NOT add textures, patterns, or noise.
• DO NOT use pastel, muted, or overly soft color palettes.
• DO NOT make everything symmetrical—kinetic minimalism requires subtle asymmetry.

The output must always reflect true Kinetic Minimalism:
clean minimal surfaces, dynamic motion cues, bold directional accents, energetic composition, and crisp modern typography.
```
